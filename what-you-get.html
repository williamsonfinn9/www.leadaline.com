What you get with LeadaLine

Every piece of the system working together — not a list of tools, but a lead-response setup built around your business.

🤖

AI Lead Assistant

What it does
Responds to inbound enquiries from your website or landing page, asks the right qualification questions and collects the details your team needs to follow up.
Why it matters
Customers are not left waiting for a reply. They get an immediate, professional response — even when you are on a job, in a meeting or asleep.
✓ Cleaner, more useful enquiries from the start
🌐

Website or Landing Page Integration

What it does
LeadaLine connects to your existing website or, if needed, a dedicated landing page is built to capture enquiries more effectively.
Why it matters
Many service business websites are not built to convert. LeadaLine either improves the existing setup or creates the right entry point for the lead flow.
✓ More enquiries captured from your existing traffic
❓

Lead Qualification Flow

What it does
A set of qualification questions tailored to your services and business — covering service type, location, urgency, budget range and contact preference.
Why it matters
Not all enquiries are equal. The qualification flow helps you prioritise the most urgent or valuable leads and respond accordingly.
✓ Spend time on the right leads, not just the latest ones
📧

Owner Email Summaries

What it does
Every new qualified lead triggers a clear email summary sent directly to you — with name, service, location, urgency and contact details, all in one place.
Why it matters
You do not need to log into anything to know a lead has arrived. It arrives in your inbox, ready to act on.
✓ Respond to leads from your existing inbox
📲

SMS Owner Alerts

What it does
As well as email, a short SMS alert is sent when a new lead arrives — so you know immediately even when you are on site or away from email.
Why it matters
Email is easy to miss during a busy day. An SMS gets your attention in seconds — which is the difference between calling back first or third.
✓ Never miss a new enquiry, even when you are on a job
📊

CRM Lead Tracking

What it does
Every lead is stored in a simple CRM with status tracking — New, Contacted, Quoted, Booked or Lost. Add notes, update status, see the full history.
Why it matters
Without a system, leads get forgotten. The CRM means every enquiry has a clear status and nothing falls through the cracks.
✓ A clear view of every lead from first contact to booked work
🖥️

Client Dashboard

What it does
A simple, clean dashboard where you can view all current leads, update statuses, add notes and see how enquiries are moving through the pipeline.
Why it matters
You get a real-time picture of your lead pipeline — which jobs are pending, which have been quoted, which are booked.
✓ Run your lead pipeline without a spreadsheet or sticky notes
📅

Monthly Performance Summary

What it does
Each month, you receive a summary of how many enquiries were captured, how quickly they were responded to, and how many converted to quoted or booked work.
Why it matters
Most service businesses have no idea what their lead conversion looks like. A monthly summary gives you the information to make better decisions.
✓ Data to improve, not just tools to use
🤝

Setup Support & Ongoing Help

What it does
LeadaLine is set up with you — not handed to you and left. The founder is involved in setup, testing and ongoing refinement throughout the pilot period.
Why it matters
A system only works if it is set up correctly for your business. Founder-led support means it gets done properly and improved based on real results.
✓ A system that is built around your business, not a generic template
📞

Optional: AI Warm-Lead Follow-Up Calling

At a later stage, LeadaLine can include an AI calling component to follow up with leads who have already enquired but not yet been reached. This is for warm inbound leads only — not cold outreach.

This feature is being developed for future pilot clients. Ask about availability during your demo call.
