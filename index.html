The fix

How LeadaLine fixes it

Four clear steps that turn an enquiry into a trackable lead — without the owner needing to be available 24/7.

1

Capture the enquiry

The AI assistant picks up enquiries from your website, form, chat or landing page — day or night, before someone goes elsewhere.

2

Qualify the customer

It asks the right questions: service needed, location, urgency, budget range and contact details. No missed information.

3

Send the owner a clear summary

You receive an email and SMS with everything you need to follow up — clearly laid out, no hunting through email threads.

4

Track the lead in the CRM

Every enquiry is logged in a simple dashboard with status, urgency and notes. You can see every lead from new to booked.
