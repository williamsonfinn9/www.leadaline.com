The system

How LeadaLine works

Six simple stages that take a customer enquiry from first contact through to a tracked, actionable lead — without the owner having to chase.

1
Step 1

Capture

Customer enquiries can arrive through a website form, a chat widget, a dedicated landing page, or a missed call trigger. LeadaLine picks them up regardless of when they come in.

Why the owner cares: Enquiries do not wait. If a customer does not get a response within a few minutes, they move on to the next business. LeadaLine ensures every enquiry gets picked up immediately.

Example

"A visitor lands on a roofing company's website at 9pm. They fill in the enquiry form. LeadaLine picks it up instantly."

2
Step 2

Qualify

The AI assistant asks practical questions about the service needed, location, urgency, preferred contact method and any relevant details. The questions are tailored to your business and services.

Why the owner cares: You get cleaner, more useful enquiries — not just a name and email. You know whether the job is worth calling back immediately or scheduling for later.

Example

"The assistant asks: What type of roofing work is needed? What is the property postcode? Is this urgent or planned work? Best contact number?"

3
Step 3

Summarise

Once the enquiry is captured, the system turns the conversation into a structured, readable lead summary. The owner receives the key details in a clear format — no raw transcript, no noise.

Why the owner cares: You can read the summary in 30 seconds and know exactly what to say when you call the customer back. No hunting through emails or forms to piece together what they wanted.

Example output

"Name: James Smith. Service: Ridge tile repair. Location: M21, South Manchester. Urgency: High — water ingress. Phone: 07xxx xxxxxx. Best time: Afternoon."

4
Step 4

Notify

The business owner receives an email and an SMS alert with the lead summary. The alert arrives within seconds of the enquiry being completed — so you can respond while the customer is still thinking about you.

Why the owner cares: Speed matters. A business that calls back within five minutes is far more likely to win the job than one that calls back the next day. The alert means you can react immediately.

Example SMS alert

"New lead: James Smith — ridge tile repair, South Manchester, urgent. Check your dashboard for full details."

5
Step 5

Track

Every lead is stored in a simple CRM dashboard. You can see lead status (New, Contacted, Quoted, Booked, Lost), urgency, notes and enquiry date. Nothing gets lost in an inbox.

Why the owner cares: Most service businesses have no idea how many enquiries they receive, how many they respond to, or how many they win. LeadaLine gives you a clear picture of every lead.

Example dashboard view

"This week: 12 new enquiries. 9 contacted. 5 quoted. 3 booked. 2 awaiting follow-up."

6
Step 6

Improve

Monthly performance summaries help the business understand how many leads were captured, how quickly they were responded to, and how the enquiry flow can be improved over time.

Why the owner cares: Over time, the system gets tuned to your business. Qualification questions improve. The dashboard evolves. You get more from each month than the last.

Example monthly summary

"May: 48 leads captured. Average response time: 4 minutes. 22 converted to quoted work. Busiest day: Tuesday. Top service: boiler repairs."

Less chasing. More customers.
